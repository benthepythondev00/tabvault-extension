# Privacy Policy for TabVault

**Last Updated: December 2025**

## Overview

TabVault is a browser extension that helps you save and restore browser tab sessions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect
TabVault collects and stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- **Tab URLs and titles**: To save and restore your sessions
- **Tab group information**: Group names, colors, and collapsed state
- **Window positions**: To restore window layouts (optional)
- **Session metadata**: Names, colors, tags, and timestamps you assign

### What We Do NOT Collect
- Personal identification information
- Browsing history beyond saved sessions
- Cookies or login credentials
- Any data from the content of web pages
- Analytics or tracking data

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API:
- Sessions and settings are stored in `chrome.storage.local`
- Data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it
- Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data

## Third-Party Services

### ExtensionPay
If you choose to upgrade to Pro, we use ExtensionPay for payment processing. ExtensionPay may collect:
- Email address (for account and receipt)
- Payment information (processed by Stripe)

ExtensionPay's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy at https://extensionpay.com/privacy

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. The only data that leaves your device is:
- Payment information when upgrading to Pro (handled by ExtensionPay/Stripe)
- Data you explicitly export using the Export feature

## Your Rights

You have full control over your data:
- **Export**: Download all your data as a JSON file at any time
- **Delete**: Remove any session or all data through the extension
- **Uninstall**: Removing the extension deletes all stored data

## Permissions Explained

TabVault requests the following permissions:

- **storage**: To save your sessions locally
- **tabs**: To read and create browser tabs
- **tabGroups**: To save and restore Chrome tab groups

These permissions are used solely for the stated functionality and nothing else.

## Children's Privacy

TabVault is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store support page or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
