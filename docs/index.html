TabVault

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what TabVault does with your data. The short version: your tab data stays in your own browser. TabVault has no server and no analytics, and we never receive your sessions.

Who we are

TabVault is a Chrome extension made and maintained by an independent developer (GitHub: benthepythondev00). It is available on the Chrome Web Store (extension ID abnclgimbelipkonifmbdbkkelloadmf).

What we collect

TabVault stores the following on your device, in your browser only:

  • Saved sessions: for each session you save, the web address (URL) and title of each tab, the address of each tab's icon (favicon), whether the tab is pinned, its position, your tab groups (name, color, collapsed or not) and your window layout (which tabs are in which window, plus window size, position and state).
  • Session details you add: session names, colors, tags and workspace names.
  • Backups: copies of your saved sessions that TabVault creates before a restore or a delete, when you ask for one, and automatically on a timer if you are a Pro user and have automatic backups on.
  • Settings: your theme, backup interval and retention, and restore preferences.
  • Usage counters: a simple per-day count of sessions and tabs saved and restored, kept for the last 30 days and shown only to you inside the extension.

We do not collect your name, your email address, your browsing history beyond the tabs in sessions you save, cookies, passwords or the content of web pages. TabVault has no analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

Why we collect it

This data exists so you can save a set of tabs and get it back later, and so a backup is there if you restore or delete something by mistake. TabVault does not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

  • Sessions, backups, settings and counters are stored in Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device.
  • If you press Export in the settings, TabVault creates a JSON file with your data and your browser saves it where you choose. The file is yours, and TabVault does not read it again until you import it.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or share your tab data, and nothing in your sessions is uploaded to any server, including ours.

ExtensionPay (Pro upgrade)

TabVault is free to use with a limit of 5 saved sessions, and has an optional Pro upgrade. Payments are handled by ExtensionPay. The extension contacts ExtensionPay to check whether Pro is active, using a random license key that is not linked to your browsing. If you upgrade or log in, ExtensionPay receives your email address, and Stripe processes your payment details. We never see your card details. Your tabs and sessions are never sent to ExtensionPay.

The Chrome Web Store distributes the extension, and Google's own privacy policy covers the store itself.

How long we keep it

  • Saved sessions: until you delete them.
  • Backups: TabVault removes backups older than your retention setting (7 days by default) and keeps at most the 10 most recent per session.
  • Usage counters: the last 30 days.
  • Exported files: until you delete them. They are ordinary files on your computer.
  • When you uninstall: Chrome removes all of TabVault's in-browser data. Exported files stay until you delete them.

Your rights and choices

Since your data lives on your own device, you are in control of it.

  • Access: you can see your sessions in the TabVault popup, and download everything as a JSON file with Export in the settings.
  • Deletion: delete any session in the popup, or uninstall TabVault to remove all in-browser data at once.
  • Portability: Import restores an exported file into TabVault.
  • Payment data: for questions about your ExtensionPay account or payment data, use the links on ExtensionPay's privacy page.

We never hold a copy of your tab data, so we cannot look it up or delete it for you, but we are happy to help you find it.

Permissions explained

  • storage: to save your sessions in your browser.
  • tabs: to read the tabs you save and to open them when you restore.
  • tabGroups: to save and restore Chrome tab groups.

TabVault also runs a small script on extensionpay.com pages only, so the extension learns when a payment is complete. It reads nothing else.

Children

TabVault is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and it sends no tab data off your device.

Changes to this policy

If TabVault's data use changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.

Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy: use the support tab of the TabVault Chrome Web Store listing, or open an issue on GitHub.
