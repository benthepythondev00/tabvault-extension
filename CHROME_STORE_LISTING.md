## Detailed Description

**TabVault - The Tab Session Manager That Actually Works**

Frustrated with losing your research tabs? Tired of session managers that randomly delete your data? TabVault was built to solve these problems.

**WHY TABVAULT?**

Unlike other session managers, TabVault:
- NEVER silently overwrites your sessions
- Creates automatic backups before every operation
- Fully supports Chrome tab groups (colors, names, collapsed state)
- Has a simple, intuitive interface

**FREE FEATURES**

- Save up to 5 browser sessions
- Full tab group support
- Search across all your saved sessions
- Export/Import your data (JSON backup)
- Light and Dark themes
- Works offline - all data stored locally

**PRO FEATURES ($2.99/month or $29 lifetime)**

- Unlimited saved sessions
- Workspaces to organize sessions
- Automatic background backups
- Session history with undo
- Priority email support

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Click the TabVault icon
2. Click "Save Session" to capture all your current tabs
3. Name it and choose a color
4. Later, click the play button to restore all tabs instantly

**PERFECT FOR**

- Researchers with multiple project tabs
- Developers juggling different projects
- Students organizing study materials
- Anyone who uses lots of tabs

**PRIVACY FOCUSED**

All your data stays on YOUR computer. We never collect or transmit your browsing data. See our privacy policy for details.

## Additional Information

### Why This Extension?
Built after researching competitor pain points. Tab Session Manager (100k users, 3.5 stars) has major complaints about data loss and missing tab groups support. TabVault addresses these directly.

### Permissions Justification
- storage: Required to save sessions locally
- tabs: Required to read current tabs and create new tabs on restore
- tabGroups: Required to save and restore Chrome's native tab groups
